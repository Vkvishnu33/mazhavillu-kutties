Privacy policy: Mazhavillu Uploader

Last updated: 18 October 2026

What it is

The Mazhavillu Uploader is a small program on the channel owner's own computer. It uploads the Mazhavillu Kutties channel's own videos to YouTube, with their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists. Nobody else uses it, and it works only for this one channel.

YouTube API Services

The program uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to what YouTube and Google do with data.

What data it uses

  • It signs in to the channel's own Google account, with the owner's permission, to upload videos and manage that channel's videos and playlists.
  • It reads back the channel's own videos and playlists, only to check that an upload went right.
  • It does not read, collect or keep any data about viewers, subscribers, commenters or anyone else.

Where it is kept, and who it is shared with

  • The sign-in is kept only on the owner's computer. It is never put on a website or a server, and never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • The videos go straight from the owner's computer to YouTube.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, and there are no adverts or tracking in the program or on this site.

How to remove its access

The channel's owner can withdraw the program's access at any time in the Google Account's third-party connections page (Google Account, then Security, then third-party access). Deleting the program's sign-in file on the computer also ends it.
